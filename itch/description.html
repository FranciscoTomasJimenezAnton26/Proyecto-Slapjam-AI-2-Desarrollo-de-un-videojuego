Kid or monster? Decide FAST! 🎃🔔

It's Halloween night and the doorbell won't stop ringing. Trick-or-treaters are lining up at your door… but some of those “costumes” aren't costumes at all.

Serve 15 visitors as fast as you can:

  • 🍬 Swipe RIGHT to give candy to real kids
  • 🚪 Swipe LEFT to slam the door on monsters
  • 💡 Tap the porch light to reveal their true shadow

Look for the clue: a wolf tail, glowing eyes, floating feet, claws, fangs, little horns, a tentacle, furry paws…

⚡ Speed is everything

  • ⏱️ It's a speedrun: your final time is your score
  • 🍭 Chain correct calls to trigger a SUGAR RUSH: visitors arrive faster, the music speeds up and your time drops
  • ⏳ Visitors don't wait: hesitate and a monster lets itself in
  • ❌ Every mistake adds +3 seconds
  • 🏆 Earn your rank, from D · Haunted House to S · King of Halloween, and beat your best time. Live splits show whether you're ahead of your record.

📱 Best played on your phone, in portrait. On desktop: ← → arrow keys to swipe, Space for the light.

🎯 Theme: SPEED

Doorbell Dash is a speedrun disguised as a Halloween night: the clock never stops, every streak makes the night go faster, and every second of doubt costs you.

  • Claude (Anthropic): game design, all the code (Phaser 3), the sound design and the tools used to process the art and audio
  • Google Gemini (Nano Banana): all 16 characters (8 kids and their monster versions), the porch, the hallway and the door
  • Google Gemini (Lyria): music
